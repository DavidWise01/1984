The story engine

01 · Observe

Move through original sprite locations using WASD, arrow keys or touch controls.

02 · Encounter

Inspect objects, characters and historical claims.

03 · Interpret

Separate observable fact from the official narrative and Winston's inference.

04 · Preserve

Record a chapter without rewriting previously committed story episodes.

05 · Emerge

Advance exactly one chapter per release; each chapter has independent HTML and a ledger entry.

The scope

Part One: 8 chapters. Part Two: 10 chapters. Part Three: 6 chapters. 24 chapters total. This index unlocks only chapters that have actually been authored and committed.

● Chapters 01–13 committed

The story is based on the novel's themes and plot. Narration, graphics and interaction are independently written rather than copied from the novel.
